## Overview

Financial system includes individual and institutional investors, markets and intermediaries, ultimate borrowers, and lastly the government which supervises and regulates the markets and institutions. The main role of a financial system is to channel funds from the savers to borrowers and facilitate investment and funding decisions. Investment decisions involve many layers, such as the time value of money and risk associated with investments’ potential payoffs.

Risk pricing is a quintessential feature of a wider-ranging family of financial investments. The course examines the information that investors need to consider to price financial assets including potential payoffs and likelihoods of the potential payoffs. Depending on each investor’s attitude towards risk, such considerations lead to different valuations of the same investment. The course continue by defining the various types of financial intermediaries, their typical balance sheet and risks they face. We then describe a framework that relies on our learning from financial returns and construction of certainty equivalent to quantify the amount of liquidity provided by the intermediation sector across the financial system and discuss the implication of decisions made at the intermediation sector on availability of credit and shareholder valuation.







### Course Contents
The course is organised according to theories and empirical facts related to financial markets and institutions. Both aspects are essential in terms of understanding the course material and examinations but also in terms of their importance towards developing a foundation for future careers in finance within or outside academia. ### Topic 1: Foundations of Asset Pricing
Overview of financial assets, return measurement, risk and uncertainty, discounting, present value, market efficiency, and the fundamental principles of asset valuation *(Google Colab, Pandas, NumPy, yfinance; Datasets: CRSP, Yahoo Finance; AI: ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot).*

### Topic 2: Mean–Variance Analysis and Portfolio Theory
Portfolio returns and risk, diversification, efficient frontier, mean–variance optimisation, portfolio selection, and the Capital Allocation Line *(NumPy, SciPy, CVXPY, PyPortfolioOpt; Datasets: CRSP, Kenneth French Data Library; AI: ChatGPT, Claude).*

### Topic 3: Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)
Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), beta estimation, Security Market Line (SML), expected returns, systematic and idiosyncratic risk, and empirical testing *(Statsmodels, Scikit-learn; Datasets: CRSP, Fama–French Factors; AI: ChatGPT, Perplexity AI).*

### Topic 4: Arbitrage Pricing Theory (APT) and Multifactor Models
Arbitrage Pricing Theory, macroeconomic risk factors, factor construction, multifactor models, and empirical estimation *(Statsmodels, linearmodels; Datasets: CRSP–Compustat (CCM), Kenneth French Data Library; AI: ChatGPT, Claude).*

### Topic 5: Factor Investing and Style Factors
Value, size, momentum, profitability, investment, quality, low volatility, smart beta, and factor portfolio construction *(Pandas, Statsmodels, PyPortfolioOpt; Datasets: Kenneth French Data Library, AQR Data Library; AI: ChatGPT, Perplexity AI).*

### Topic 6: Time-Series and Cross-Sectional Asset Pricing
Time-series regression, cross-sectional regression, Fama–MacBeth estimation, panel data models, and empirical asset pricing techniques *(Statsmodels, linearmodels; Datasets: CRSP, Compustat; AI: ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot).*

### Topic 7: Market Efficiency and Asset Pricing Anomalies
Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH), calendar effects, momentum, reversals, post-earnings announcement drift, and other pricing anomalies *(Pandas, Statsmodels; Datasets: CRSP, Kenneth French Data Library; AI: ChatGPT, Claude).*

### Topic 8: Behavioural Asset Pricing
Behavioural finance, investor sentiment, overconfidence, limits to arbitrage, prospect theory, and behavioural explanations of asset prices *(Pandas, Matplotlib; Datasets: Investor Sentiment Data, Kenneth French Data Library; AI: ChatGPT, Perplexity AI).*

### Topic 9: Fixed Income and Bond Pricing
Bond pricing, yield curves, duration, convexity, term structure models, interest rate risk, and fixed-income portfolio management *(QuantLib, SciPy; Datasets: FRED, U.S. Treasury Yield Curve Data; AI: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot).*

### Topic 10: Derivative Securities and Option Pricing
Forward contracts, futures, options, Black–Scholes model, binomial pricing, Greeks, and risk-neutral valuation *(QuantLib, NumPy, SciPy; Datasets: OptionMetrics, CBOE Options Data; AI: ChatGPT, Claude).*

### Topic 11: Empirical Asset Pricing and Contemporary Research
Empirical testing of pricing models, model comparison, out-of-sample evaluation, factor model performance, reproducible research, and current developments in quantitative asset pricing *(Statsmodels, linearmodels, PyPortfolioOpt; Datasets: WRDS (CRSP, Compustat, IBES), Kenneth French Data Library; AI: ChatGPT, NotebookLM, GitHub Copilot).*
